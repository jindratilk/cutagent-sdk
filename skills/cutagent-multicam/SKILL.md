# Multicam

Identify every camera, microphone, speaker, and intended programme audio source. Establish sync from trustworthy waveform, slate, timecode, or visible event evidence; filenames and similar durations are insufficient. Check alignment near the start and later in the sequence for drift.

Inspect the installed SDK or public CutAgent CLI command cards for the exact multicam operations and current limits. Confirm angle/source identity and offsets before switching. Keep the programme audio stable while changing picture. Verify cuts around overlaps, reactions, and late-sequence sync; record unresolved drift rather than presenting one aligned point as complete qualification.
