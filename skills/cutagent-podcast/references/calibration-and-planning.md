# Podcast calibration and planning

Use this after synchronization and before applying an automatic speaker plan.

Review representative beginning, middle, and end sections for speech level, noise floor, crosstalk, laughter, short interjections, simultaneous speech, and long pauses. A useful activity signal distinguishes intentional speech from leakage without turning every syllable into a camera cut.

Tune the plan for the programme's character:

- relaxed interviews need longer holds and fewer reaction cuts;
- energetic debate can cut sooner but still needs readable turns;
- educational demonstrations should stay on the angle that explains the action;
- emotional stories should protect pauses and listener reactions;
- true overlap usually benefits from a wide or stable two-shot.

Review the complete plan in bounded sections. Check the opening angle, average/minimum holds, cuts per minute, suppressed short events, overlaps, uncovered ranges, and final angle. Regenerate through supported planner inputs when the rhythm is weak; do not patch opaque generated coordinates.

Use transcript evidence to interpret who is speaking and why a turn matters. Acoustic evidence remains the authority for actual speech activity and listening remains the authority for editorial rhythm.
