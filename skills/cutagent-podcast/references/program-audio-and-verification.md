# Podcast programme audio and verification

Choose programme tracks by speaker role. Preserve the proven microphone/person mapping and the synchronized source relationship for every placed section.

Use continuous isolated tracks when the recording is clean and stable. Use activity-shaped slices when leakage or overlap needs deliberate control. In either case, preserve real interjections and laughter, add unobtrusive fades at new boundaries, and avoid doubling embedded scratch audio.

Check opening, dense dialogue, a genuine overlap, a quiet passage, and the ending. At each section verify:

- the active camera and intended speaker relationship;
- the actual microphone source and programme track;
- continuous room tone with no clicks, gaps, echo, or doubled scratch;
- stable sync at early, middle, and late points;
- valid source range through the final frame.

If media inside a native multicam is replaced, verify both the internal source identity and playback from an outer edited timeline. Readback proves structure; listening proves the programme sound.
